A restore can’t make things worse.

Both dates

When a file is in the way, Backtrack shows when each copy was last changed, and which is newer, before you choose.

Keep Both

The backup’s version takes the name, and yours stays beside it with “(current)” added.

Nothing deleted

At home on GNOME and KDE.

Also Hyprland and COSMIC and Xfce and Cinnamon and MATE and Sway and ...

Files and Dolphin

GNOME Files and Dolphin both get four items. Rewind This File… and Restore Previous Version… are on a file’s right-click menu, and Rewind This Folder… and Browse Backups of This Folder… are on a folder’s, or on the empty space inside one. In Dolphin they sit in a Backtrack submenu.

The tray

When something needs you, the icon says Backups need your attention. Its menu starts with how things stand, and has Rewind…, Open Backtrack, Back Up Now and Pause Backups.

The terminal

The backtrack command checks, backs up, pauses, searches and restores. Add --json to status or search for output a script can read.

$ backtrack status
Your files are backed up.

  Last backup   2026-11-05 14:00:04Z (12 minutes ago)
  Next backup   2026-11-05 15:00:00Z (in 48 minutes)
  Destination   reachable

$ backtrack search boarding   # finds it in every backup
$ backtrack pause 2h          # no backups for two hours
$ backtrack backup-now        # a backup, right now

Keyboard and Orca

Every control has a name a screen reader can read out. Backtrack follows your desktop’s light or dark style, and high contrast too. The shortcuts worth knowing:

  • Ctrl+FSearch every backup
  • Ctrl+TRewind
  • Ctrl+RRestore
  • Ctrl+DCompare with Today
  • Ctrl+HShow hidden files
09:00Backup 6 of 13

Built on Borg, and yours to keep.

  • Your backups are BorgBackup repositories, encrypted on this computer before they leave it and deduplicated, so each hour stores only what changed.
  • If Backtrack vanished tomorrow, Borg alone could still open them.
  • When you set it up, you save a recovery key: a copy of your backups’ key. If the copy kept with the backups is ever damaged, the recovery key and your passphrase open them again.
  • Backtrack has no telemetry. Your backups go where you send them, and nothing else leaves your computer.
  • It’s free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later, and all of it is on GitHub.

Your files, not your system.

What Backtrack keeps

Your documents, pictures, music, downloads and desktop, with your app settings and your SSH and GPG keys, every hour. You can pick other folders instead.

  • Documents
  • Pictures
  • Music
  • Downloads
  • Desktop
  • App settings
  • SSH and GPG keys

What it leaves alone

The system itself: its programs, settings and updates. Timeshift looks after that, or Snapper on btrfs. If you use Snapper, Backtrack shows its snapshots in the timeline too.

  • Programs
  • System settings
  • Updates
Wed 15:00Backup 8 of 13

Questions people ask first.

Can it open my Vorta or Pika Backup backups?
Yes. Choose Already have backups? when you set it up and point it at them. It leaves their own backups alone.
Where can my backups go?
A USB drive, a network folder, or a server over SSH.
Will it run on my computer?
Yes, if it runs GNOME, KDE Plasma, Hyprland, COSMIC, Xfce, Cinnamon, MATE or Sway. There are packages for Fedora 44 and later, Ubuntu 26.04 and later and Arch Linux, and the Flatpak runs on any distribution.
